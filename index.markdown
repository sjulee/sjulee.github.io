I am an Assistant Professor of Analytics and Operations Management at the Freeman College of Management at Bucknell University. My research interests are in healthcare applications of optimization and machine learning, specifically in designing interpretable data-driven optimization frameworks and tractable solution methods to solve problems in disease prediction and medical decision-making.

I received my Ph.D. from the H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering at Georgia Institute of Technology, advised by [Dr. Gian-Gabriel Garcia](https://giangabrielgarcia.com/).
