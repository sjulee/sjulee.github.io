# Teaching

## Bucknell University
### ANOP 102 Spreadsheet Modeling & Data Analysis
This course serves as the introduction to quantitative modeling and basic statistical analysis in a spreadsheet-based environment, especially as they apply to managerial decision making.


### ANOP 370 Prescriptive Analytics: Decision Modeling & Optimization
An introduction to decision modeling and analysis using deterministic optimization models and solution methodologies.
