# Research

## Research Interests  
I study problems at the intersection of **optimization, machine learning, and data-driven decision-making**, with applications in healthcare. A central theme of my work is developing models that are **interpretable, fair, and robust**, so that predictive and prescriptive tools can be reliably applied in high-stakes environments.  

## Methods  
- Data-driven optimization  
- Robust and stochastic optimization  
- Markov decision processes  
- Predictive modeling and machine learning
